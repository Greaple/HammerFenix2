    THE CAVERN FIELD GUIDE

    A few things to remember.

    Jump through platforms from below. A bomb freezes nearby enemies and sends them sliding. Bump frozen fruit into other enemies for a chain reaction.

    Move← → or A / D (Q / D)
    Jump↑ or W / Z
    Drop / kick a bombSPACE or Ctrl / X
    Kick upward↓ + SPACE
    Pause / resumeP or Esc

    One bomb at a time. Drop one in midair for a small upward boost. Falling below an uncleared room brings you back at the top; once it's clear, falling takes you deeper.

    Touch controls appear on smaller screens. Standard gamepads: D-pad / left stick to move, A to jump, B to bomb, Start to pause.

    A SECOND LIFE FOR HAMMERFEST

    Old soul. New foundations.

    HammerFenix is an independent recreation of Motion Twin's Hammerfest, originally released in 2006. This preview brings its opening ten combat rooms to the modern web.

    The original backgrounds, animation frames, music, and level layouts are preserved. Movement uses the original timing and physics constants.

    This is the first playable milestone.

    Enemy decision-making and scoring are simplified. Quests, secret dimensions, special items, the introduction, and later levels are still to be ported. The full decoded level archive is included in the source.
